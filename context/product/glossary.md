# Glossary

## Term

A real word form or phrase encountered in text.

Examples:

- `sleep`
- `slept`
- `went`
- `take off`

## LingQ

A saved learning term with a meaning and context. In reader UI, LingQs are yellow.

## Known Term

A real term the user has marked as known. In reader UI, known terms are white/default text.

## Ignored Term

A term intentionally excluded from learning statistics and highlighting.

## Lemma

An abstract dictionary/root form. Lemmas are legacy/supporting metadata and are not the basis for new vocabulary behavior.
